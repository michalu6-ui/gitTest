Wawel in Krakow

The heart of Polish history on the Vistula River

Back to homepage Back to Krakow page Transport and airport Hotels

Wawel is a historic hill on the Vistula River in central Krakow. For centuries it was the seat of Polish rulers and an important centre of the country's political, religious and cultural life.

Key facts

10th century beginnings of settlement on the hill
Royal Castle former residence of Polish monarchs
114 m height above the Vistula

What is worth seeing?

Visit the Royal Castle, Wawel Cathedral and the Royal Tombs. Other highlights include the Sigismund Bell, the Renaissance castle courtyard and the Dragon's Den, linked to the legend of the Wawel Dragon.

Why is Wawel important?

Wawel brings together monuments from many periods, from the Middle Ages to the modern era. It is a symbol of Polish statehood and one of Krakow's most important landmarks, visited by tourists from around the world.

Public transport

  • Filharmonia - a popular tram stop by the Planty park, followed by a walk to Wawel.
  • Jubilat - a convenient tram and bus stop by Zygmunt Krasiński Avenue.
  • Stradom - an important interchange between the Old Town and Kazimierz.

Getting here from Krakow Balice Airport

  • The SKA1 train operated by Koleje Małopolskie runs to Krakow Główny station, from where you can take a tram or walk towards Wawel.
  • City buses 209 and 300 connect the airport with Krakow; night bus 902 also operates. Check current connections before travelling.
