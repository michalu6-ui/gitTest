Palace of Culture and Science

A symbol of Warsaw, science and culture

Back to homepage Transport and airports Hotels

The Palace of Culture and Science is one of Warsaw's most recognisable buildings. Completed in 1955, it remains an important centre for culture, science, business and entertainment.

Key facts

237 m height including the spire
1955 year completed
30th floor viewing terrace

What is worth seeing?

The palace houses theatres, cinemas, museums, conference rooms and concert halls. Its viewing terrace offers a panorama of Warsaw, while exhibitions, concerts and educational events take place throughout the year.

Public transport

  • Centrum Metro station - the most convenient connection from many districts, followed by a short walk.
  • Warszawa Centralna railway station - trams and buses stop nearby, just a few minutes from the palace.
  • Centrum stop - a major tram and bus interchange by Dmowskiego roundabout.

Getting here from Warsaw Modlin Airport

  • The airport shuttle bus connects to Modlin station, where trains run to Warsaw. Direct buses to central Warsaw are also available.

Getting here from Warsaw Chopin Airport

  • SKM trains S2/S30 and Koleje Mazowieckie trains connect the airport with the city centre. Get off at Warszawa Centralna or Warszawa Śródmieście and walk to the palace.
